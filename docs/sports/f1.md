## Links

- [How to Build a Car | Adrian Newey](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/37486037)
  - Autobiography of Adrian Newey, a famous F1 car designer
- [Why Work in F1 | Dan Fallows](https://danfallows.substack.com/p/why-work-in-f1)
  - For the purpose, racing, some perks and top level get paid well
  - But stress and pressure can be high, no competition, low starting salary,
    not as glamorous actually
  - Choose a job because of the job, the day-to-day-work, not the business
- Twitter:
  [Info on motorsports and aerodynamics | @dr_obbs](https://x.com/dr_obbs/status/1767849597349593261)
  - Links to many books and resources
- [The history of F1 Design](https://www.espn.com/espn/feature/story/_/id/43832710/how-f1-evolved-1950-where-headed-2026)
  - A very high level overview of F1 design, touching on aspects like chassis,
    weight, floor, wings, engine, etc.
  - There are soundtracks of V12, V10, V8 and V6 engines
  - And how these will be transitioned into 2026 regulations
- YouTube:
  [How Formula 1 Travels Across the World | Formula 1](https://youtu.be/HjrzBOnc5yU)
  (17:27)
  - How sea freight, air freight, and trucks are used to transport F1 cars and
    equipment across the world
- YouTube:
  [How Formula 1 is Filmed | Tommy Dewilt](https://youtu.be/78wxrEbuHFA) (11:31)
